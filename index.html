Abstract

I study near-field and XL-MIMO communications, adaptive wireless learning, and generative sensing and inference. My work combines physical channel models, optimization, and learning to reduce training overhead, adapt to new environments, and design informative measurements.

Index terms— Near-field & XL-MIMO; adaptive learning; generative sensing.

01 /ABOUT

I. Introduction

I am a Ph.D. student in Information Engineering at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, supervised by Prof. Ying-Jun Angela Zhang. My research connects physical modeling, information theory, optimization, and deep learning.

Across these topics, I focus on the structure of wireless environments: how radio waves propagate, how models adapt across scenarios, and how prior knowledge can guide sensing and inference.

“Show my approximate region” contacts ipwho.is only when clicked. The result is an approximate network location, which can be affected by VPNs. It is shown in this tab and is not added to a public visitor history.
When the optional analytics service is connected, it also receives section visibility, active viewing time, scroll milestones, and paper/BibTeX clicks.
